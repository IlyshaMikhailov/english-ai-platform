My best friend is named Alex. He is tall and well-built because he plays basketball every weekend. Alex has dark curly hair, cute freckles across his nose, and a small moustache.

Alex is very clever and curious. He always asks questions about science and reads difficult books in the library. He is also extremely polite and patient. When we wait in long queues at the cinema, he never gets angry and always says «thank you» to the staff. Sometimes Alex can be a bit stubborn when we argue about computer games, but he is a wonderful friend who always helps everyone.
